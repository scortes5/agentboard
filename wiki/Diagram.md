# Diagram

![Diagram](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scortes5/agentboard/main/docs/images/diagram.png)

**Diagram** (⇧⌘D) is a UML-style overview: one package per project, its groups inside, and each agent with its subscription, task, status, time working, branch and last line of output. Task queues appear as notes and arrows show who handed work to whom.

Click an agent to jump to its card; *Back to Board* or Esc closes the view.
